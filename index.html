Steve Mackey

I've always been drawn to work that lives at the intersection of design, technology and strategy. Digital work is ephemeral, and this website serves as a record of a career spent making things, some of which no longer exist out in the wild.

The work here spans interactive storytelling for media organizations, projects for cultural institutions and mission-driven organizations, and work for global brands. If you'd like to go deeper, the case studies are a good place to start.
