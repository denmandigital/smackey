Steve Mackey

Design Technologist

I work at the intersection of communications, user experience design, and digital delivery for growing companies and organizations navigating complex change. I have deep expertise in digital product development and interactive storytelling. I set product vision, align stakeholders, and lead cross-functional teams to deliver purposeful, high-impact digital experiences.

For 10 years, as a partner at Denman Digital, I built and led a team working with nonprofit, media, cultural, and public-sector organizations. Denman was acquired in early 2026. I stayed on through the transition and I'm now looking for what's next.

My work has been awarded internationally by Applied Arts, Canadian Screen Awards, Communication Arts, Digi Awards, Digital Marketing Magazine, FWA, FITC, New Media Awards, One Show Interactive, Summit International Awards, and the Webby Awards.
