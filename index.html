A foundation in creative technology.

I began my career as a hybrid designer and developer, specializing in interactive storytelling at CBC Radio 3, NFB Interactive, and several independent digital agencies. Working across creative and technical disciplines shaped my approach to problem-solving and laid the groundwork for the multidisciplinary leadership roles that followed.

Building and leading a digital agency.

As co-owner and Director of Design & Technology at Denman Digital, I spent a decade helping build and lead an award-winning agency acquired in 2026. I led digital strategy, experience design, and technical direction, setting the creative standard for work delivered to more than 50 organizations across the nonprofit, education, healthcare, media, cultural, and public sectors.

Simplifying complex challenges.

I work closely with clients and stakeholders to understand their goals, drawing on research and data to identify opportunities, inform design decisions, and measure success. From discovery and experience strategy to design systems and technical delivery, I connect big-picture thinking with practical execution.

Creating the conditions for great work.

I believe great work happens when talented people share a clear sense of purpose and direction. I bring cross-functional teams together around a common vision, fostering a collaborative culture where people feel supported, take ownership, and contribute their strengths. Through mentorship, clear direction, and constructive feedback, I help teams grow and deliver their best work.

Purpose beyond the work.

What motivates me most is helping organizations that do meaningful work succeed. I enjoy tackling complex challenges and finding ways to advance their goals, inspire their audiences, and better serve their communities. When those organizations succeed, everyone benefits.
