Steve Mackey

I work at the intersection of digital strategy, user experience design and technology, building digital products for growing companies and organizations navigating complex change. I shape product direction, align stakeholders, and lead cross-functional teams to deliver purposeful, high-impact work.

For 10 years, as managing partner at Denman Digital, I set the creative standard, connected design to business outcomes, and built and led a team working with nonprofit, healthcare, media, cultural, and public-sector organizations, as well as technology companies. Following Denman’s acquisition in early 2026, I’m looking to join a team that needs a collaborative, hands-on, creative leader.

My work has garnered recognition by Webbys and Anthem Awards, One Show Interactive, Communication Arts, Canadian Screen Awards, FWA, Applied Arts, Summit Awards, and Digital Marketing Magazine.
