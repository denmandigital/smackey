Steve Mackey

Design leader & technologist

For 25 years I've worked at the intersection of communications, user experience design and interactive storytelling, building digital products for growing companies and organizations navigating complex change. I shape product direction, align stakeholders, and lead cross-functional teams to deliver purposeful, high-impact work.

For 10 years, as managing partner at Denman Digital, I built and led a team working primarily with nonprofit, media, cultural, and public-sector organizations. Denman was acquired in early 2026 and I’m now looking for what’s next.

My work has been awarded internationally by the Webby Awards, FWA, Communication Arts, Applied Arts, Canadian Screen Awards, Summit Awards, Digi Awards, Digital Marketing Magazine, and One Show Interactive.
